About

Finance, analytics, and an international perspective

I'm a finance and business analytics professional trained at the University of Kansas, with hands-on experience across investment analysis, financial risk, procurement, consulting, and data-driven strategy.

My work sits at the intersection of three things: financial rigor (valuation, risk, financial statements), analytical tooling (SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Python), and business judgment, knowing which question actually matters and how to communicate the answer to the people making the decision.

Having grown up in Bolivia, studied in the United States, and worked with teams and vendors across continents in three languages, I bring a genuinely international perspective to business problems.
