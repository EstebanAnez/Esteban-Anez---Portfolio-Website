Professional summary

An analyst who connects finance, data, and operations

I am pursuing a dual MBA and MS in Business Analytics at the University of Kansas (expected May 2028), after earning B.S. degrees in Finance and in Business Analytics with a minor in Supply Chain Management and an Honors Program distinction. My experience spans equity research on a real student-managed portfolio, financial risk analysis at a major Bolivian bank, international procurement at a global agribusiness, venture fund analysis, and student consulting. I also build AI agents and onchain apps, from an equity research assistant to a USDC licensing app built at the University Blockchain Conference 2026, where I was also a Franklin Templeton crypto research finalist.

Across every role, the common thread is the same: take complex financial or operational data, find what matters, and communicate it so decision-makers can act on it.

I am prepared to work on problems such as:

  • Valuing companies and investments with defensible, transparent assumptions
  • Building dashboards and KPI reporting that people actually use
  • Analyzing risk, procurement, and supply chain data to support decisions
  • Structuring ambiguous business questions into a clear analytical plan
  • Presenting findings to senior stakeholders in plain language
